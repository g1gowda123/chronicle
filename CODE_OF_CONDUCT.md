# Code of conduct

Chronicle is a research project and welcomes contributors with different
backgrounds, levels of experience, and research interests.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, results, or limitations.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Assume good faith, but correct inaccurate claims with reproducible detail.
- Respect dataset licenses, privacy, and consent. Do not upload identifiable
  people or restricted video without permission.
- Keep issue threads focused; move long design discussions to a dedicated issue
  or discussion.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing private data or credentials are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's GitHub
profile or a private security advisory if the report involves sensitive data.
Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block contributors when
necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
